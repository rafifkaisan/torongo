The supplied PDFs include discussion of an earlier local-demo script, but no final video file or YouTube URL was included in the materials supplied for this repository. This is a checklist, not a claim that a video has been produced.

## Accuracy checks from the team's previous script review

1. Lead with the resource-manager user group. “Rafiq Hasan” is an **example persona**, not a real interviewed user; label it clearly as illustrative.
2. Show only the features that demonstrably work from end to end. Do not portray raster flood-area measurement, sensor fusion, population exposure, or deformation as implemented.
3. Use the NISAR plus near-date Sentinel-1 comparison section only if the team verified a suitable pair. Otherwise use a NISAR-only multi-date story and say Sentinel-1 is a comparison/history source.
4. Remove the former Community segment. Describe the in-app Explain Layer as a static reference/keyword guide, not a generative AI chatbot.
5. Do not claim landslide coverage, multiple-hazard coverage, flood prediction, early warning, or “live” flood monitoring.
6. Keep the NASA GIBS map explicitly labelled as browse imagery for visual context, not as processed water-mask evidence.
7. Show “No data available” for unimplemented measurements and explain the next processing stage without implying that it has already run.
8. Include clear English subtitles and disclose AI assistance and any AI-generated visuals/assets used in the actual video. The prior planning notes describe a fictional Rafiq Hasan persona; if its AI-generated image appears, label the character as an example persona, not a real interviewed officer.
9. Do not include identifiable names, faces, or voices of participants under 18 in the video or public project material. Do not publish guardian contact details.
10. Keep the final cut below 4:00 so a pause or credit does not push it over the limit. Check the exported YouTube video in a signed-out browser and confirm its visibility is Public or Unlisted.
